Vision

WorkPiServ is a secure freelance marketplace for the Pi Network ecosystem, allowing Pioneers to exchange professional services in Pi in a transparent and secure way.

The platform rests on three core pillars:

🤝
Trust Between client and freelancer, thanks to the built-in escrow system
🔑
Sovereignty Full control by the Pioneer over their native Pi — WorkPiServ never accesses it
📋
Transparency Of every transaction, with complete logs and documented arbitration

3
Escrow Transaction Flow

3.1 Steps of a transaction

Two kinds of movement coexist in this flow: real blockchain transactions ⛓️ (the Pi physically move) and internal accounting entries 📒 (a number simply changes in the WorkPiServ ledger; no Pi move).

1 ⛓️
TOP-UP — Real blockchain transaction The Pioneer sends Pi from their native wallet to WorkPiServ's single on-chain wallet, via the Pi Browser SDK. The backend then credits their internal balance (ledger) with the amount received.
2 📒
ORDER — Accounting entry The client uses their internal balance to order a service. No Pi move: the amount is simply marked "locked in escrow" in the ledger. The Pi stay safe, physically in WorkPiServ's single wallet since step 1.
3 📒
DELIVERY — Accounting entry The freelancer delivers the service. The client is notified to validate it. No financial movement at this step.
4A ✅
VALIDATION — Satisfied client (accounting entry) The client approves the release. The ledger splits the amount: 90% credited to the freelancer's internal balance, 10% to the WorkPiServ commission balance. The Pi always stay in the single wallet — this is the start of the safety period.
4B ⚠️
DISPUTE — Unsatisfied client Dispute opened. The Pi stay frozen in escrow (still in the single wallet) until WorkPiServ issues a transparent, documented ruling.
5 🛡️
SAFETY PERIOD — 15 days After validation (4A), the freelancer's earnings are automatically locked for 15 days before any withdrawal. The server applies this period every time funds are released, whatever the route (client validation, automatic validation, arbitration). It is a protection against late disputes, in line with freelance-market standards (see 3.3).
6 ⛓️
WITHDRAWAL — Real blockchain transaction Once the 15-day period has passed, the freelancer can request a withdrawal. A 48-hour review period (anti-fraud) precedes the payout, then WorkPiServ issues a real A2U payment from its single wallet to the freelancer's Pi account.

3.3 The 15-day safety period — why, and how it compares

WorkPiServ applies a 15-day period between the validation of an order and the availability of withdrawal. This is not a technical constraint — it is a design choice that protects both the freelancer and the client against late disputes, and it sits in the same range as industry standards:

PlatformSafety period
Fiverr (new seller)14 days after approval
Fiverr (Top Rated seller)7 days after approval
Upwork (fixed-price)5 days after approval
Upwork (hourly)10 days after the billed week
WorkPiServ15 days after validation
💡

Golden rule of escrow: WorkPiServ's single wallet physically holds the Pi of all Pioneers (available balances + active escrow). The internal ledger keeps the exact record of what belongs to whom. An automatic reconciliation periodically (every 6 hours) checks that the wallet holds at least as much Pi as the sum of all promised balances — see section 6.3.
